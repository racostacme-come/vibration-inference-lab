# Course/topic provenance

Read-only source inspection on 6 October 2026 covered all three current DTU
folders. MLSP `Books/SPT-ML - ch03.pdf`, internally titled *Discrete-Time Signals
and Systems*, develops sampled sequences and impulse responses; `SPT-ML - ch05.pdf`,
*Sampling and Quantization*, discusses uniform sampling, reconstruction and
oversampling. These concepts motivate AliasGuard's original vibration experiment.
The source material identifies the subject directly; the folder acronym was
not used to infer its meaning.

ATSA `Lectures/Lecture4_non_lin_TS.pdf` covers inference for nonlinear time series,
least squares and maximum likelihood. MMME `Notes/Lecture_4_notes_typeset.pdf`
covers heat transfer, explicit diffusion stability and interface-cell matching.
These were inspected to rotate future topics; no content from reports or solutions
was used. The series' preceding ATSA project was GapSmooth.

The connection to computational mechanics is a synthetic linear modal vibration
sensor and the ambiguity of identifying modes from downsampled measurements.
All equations, source code, synthetic parameters, plots, experiments and prose
in this repository were written for this project. No private course materials,
exercise solutions, source PDFs or datasets are distributed. Public references
in the README and manuscript support the numerical method.
